# 103 Your song

Build a song from two progressions, remove a section, shape the others, and play it.

Starts where [102](102-your-own-progressions.md) ended: Polly stopped, and a song with no sections yet.

[![103 Your song, the video (2 minutes)](../video/103.jpg)](../video/103.mp4)

The row reads "section removed".<br><br>Press <b>HOME</b> twice, for the Home page and back to the Song page. Section 4 is gone.</td><td><img src="img/103/2-remove.jpg" alt="Section 4 struck through, reading section removed, Prog on –" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>3. Shape a section</h3>Turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Section) to section 2. Turn <b>KNOB 3</b> (Groove) to B, the busier groove, and <b>KNOB 4</b> (Repeat) to x2, so it plays twice.</td><td><img src="img/103/2-shape.jpg" alt="Section 2: P2 Ballad on Groove B, x2" width="100%"></td></tr>

Press black keys 1, 2 and 3 to mute D1, D2 and PC, the drums and percussion.<br><br>They rest in section 1 only. Its row shows an M.</td><td><img src="img/103/3-mute.jpg" alt="Section 1 with an M on its row: D1, D2 and PC muted" width="100%"></td></tr>

Check the Intro and the Ending</h3>Turn <b>SELECT</b> to page 2. <b>KNOB 2</b> (Intro) and <b>KNOB 3</b> (Ending) are on, so the song starts with the style's Intro and ends with its Ending.<br><br><b>KNOB 4</b> (At end) is on stop: the song stops after the Ending.</td><td><img src="img/103/4-page2.jpg" alt="Page 2: Intro on, Ending on, At end stop" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>6. Play the song</h3>Press <b>PLAY</b>. The Intro plays first, and its clock fills at the top.<br><br>Then each section plays in turn, and its clock fills as it plays. After section 3, the Ending plays and the song stops.</td><td><img src="img/103/5-play.jpg" alt="Section 2 playing, its clock filling" width="100%"></td></tr>
