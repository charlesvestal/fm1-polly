# 106 Your own grooves

Record an instrument into the style's groove, and Polly keeps an edited copy of the groove.

Picks up where [105](105-recording-a-melody.md) left off, with Polly stopped in your song and Pop as the style.

[![Watch 106 Your own grooves (1 minute)](../video/106.jpg)](../video/106.mp4)
