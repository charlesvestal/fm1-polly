# 106 Your own grooves

Record an instrument into the style's groove, and the groove becomes yours.

[![106 Your own grooves, the video (1 minute)](../video/106.jpg)](../video/106.mp4)

<table>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>1. Pick an instrument</h3>Start with a new song: press <b>HOME</b> for the Song page, press <b>SEL</b>, turn <b>SELECT</b> to NEW SONG and press <b>SEL</b> twice. Press <b>HOME</b> to go back.<br><br>Press <b>PLAY</b>. Press <b>SEQ</b> for the Band page, and turn <b>ALGORITHM</b> to C2, Clean Guitar.<br><br>Its row is empty: it plays nothing in this groove. The keys play its sound.</td><td><img src="img/106/1-instrument.jpg" alt="The Band page with C2 Clean Guitar selected, its row empty" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>2. Record</h3>Press <b>REC</b>. Groove A becomes your own copy, Groove A (yours). Play from the next bar.<br><br>The groove loops, and each pass adds to what you played. Press <b>REC</b> again to stop recording.</td><td><img src="img/106/2-record.jpg" alt="REC C2 pass 1 in Groove A (yours)" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>3. Make it longer</h3>Turn <b>SELECT</b> to the Groove length page. Turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Bars) to 4. The new bars repeat the groove's two bars.</td><td><img src="img/106/3-length.jpg" alt="Groove length: Bars at 4" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>4. Change the style</h3>Press <b>HOME</b> for the Home page, and turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Style) to Funk. Polly asks "GROOVE A IS YOURS: KEEP IT?"<br><br>Press <b>SEL</b> to keep it. Your Groove A plays with Funk's band.</td><td><img src="img/106/4-style.jpg" alt="Home: GROOVE A IS YOURS: KEEP IT?" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>5. Give the groove back</h3>Press <b>PLAY</b> to stop. Press <b>SEQ</b>, then <b>SEL</b> for the list. Turn <b>SELECT</b> to BACK TO STYLE and press <b>SEL</b>.<br><br>Groove A plays Funk's groove again. Your copy stays in Memory.</td><td><img src="img/106/5-back.jpg" alt="The list on BACK TO STYLE" width="100%"></td></tr>
</table>
