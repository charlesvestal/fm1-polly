# Polly course

Short lessons, each with a video and a picture for every step. Take them in order. For every detail, see the
[manual](../../MANUAL.md).

The videos start from a new Polly. To start a lesson on an empty song, hold **HOME** and turn **PRESETS** to a
song you haven't used yet.

| Lesson | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| [Quick start](../../QUICKSTART.md) | Write a verse and a chorus, and play them as a song. |
| [101 Playing live](101-playing-live.md) | Pick a style, play chords, change grooves, and end with the Ending. |
| [102 Your own progressions](102-your-own-progressions.md) | Record chords, fix one, change the length, name it, and switch progressions. |
| [103 Your song](103-your-song.md) | Add and remove sections, set their grooves, repeats and mutes, and play the song. |
| [104 Changing styles](104-changing-styles.md) | Play your song in another style, and choose its tempo. |
| [105 Recording a melody](105-recording-a-melody.md) | Record a melody along the song, add to one section, and record a second melody. |
| [106 Your own grooves](106-your-own-grooves.md) | Record an instrument into a groove, make it longer, and give it back to the style. |
| [107 Fixing notes](107-fixing-notes.md) | Change, move, delete and enter single notes. |
| [108 Making it sound yours](108-making-it-sound-yours.md) | Change a sound, mix it, set the effects, and transpose. |
| [109 Keeping your work](109-keeping-your-work.md) | Save, undo, switch songs, free Memory, and back up. |
| [110 With other gear](110-with-other-gear.md) | Send MIDI out, follow MIDI clock, play from a keyboard, and mirror the screen. |
