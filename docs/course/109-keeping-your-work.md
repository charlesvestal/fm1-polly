# 109 Keeping your work

Save, undo, switch songs, free a place in Memory, and back up to your computer.

Picks up where [108](108-making-it-sound-yours.md) left off, with Polly stopped and your grooves and melodies from 105 to 108 in Memory.

[![Watch 109 Keeping your work (1 minute)](../video/109.jpg)](../video/109.mp4)

<table>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>1. Save</h3>Press <b>EDIT</b> and turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Sound) one step. <b>SAVE</b> lights up, and the header says "unsaved".<br><br>Press <b>SAVE</b>. When Polly is stopped, it also saves by itself after about four seconds.</td><td><img src="img/109/1-unsaved.jpg" alt="A new sound for C1: SAVE lit, unsaved in the header" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>2. Undo and redo</h3>Hold <b>HOME</b> and press <b>REC</b>. The old sound comes back, and the header says "UNDO C1 SOUND".<br><br>Hold <b>HOME</b> and press <b>PLAY</b> to redo it.</td><td><img src="img/109/2-undo.jpg" alt="HOME held, REC pressed: UNDO C1 SOUND" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>3. Switch songs</h3>Hold <b>HOME</b> and turn <b>PRESETS</b> to switch between songs S1 to S4. The header shows which song you're on. Turn back to S1.<br><br>Each song has its own sections, progressions, melodies, tempo and style.</td><td><img src="img/109/3-song.jpg" alt="HOME held, PRESETS turned: SONG S2" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>4. Open Memory</h3>Press <b>SEQ</b>, then <b>SEL</b>. Turn <b>SELECT</b> down to MEMORY at the bottom, and press <b>SEL</b>.<br><br>Memory has 16 places, which keep your own grooves and melodies for all four songs. The header tells you how many are in use.</td><td><img src="img/109/4-memory.jpg" alt="Memory: S1's Groove A (yours), S1's melodies and a groove no song plays, 3 of 16 used" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>5. Free a place</h3>Turn <b>SELECT</b> to the third row, numbered 2. It holds your copy from 106, which no song plays. Press <b>SEL</b>, and Polly asks if you're sure. Press <b>SEL</b> again.<br><br>The place is now free, and the copy's notes are gone.</td><td><img src="img/109/5-free.jpg" alt="Memory: FREE MEMORY 2? NOTES CLEARED" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2" valign="top"><h3>6. Back up</h3>Stop Polly, and connect the FM-1 to your computer with a USB cable. Open the <a href="https://charlesvestal.github.io/fm1-polly/backup/">Backup page</a> in Chrome or Edge, and press <b>Back up</b>. The page saves your songs to a file.</td></tr>
<tr><td colspan="2" valign="top"><h3>7. Put it back</h3>Stop Polly. On the Backup page, choose your file and press <b>Restore</b>. The backup replaces everything on Polly.<br><br>When Polly shows "RESTORE FROM USB?", press <b>SEL</b>.</td></tr>
</table>
