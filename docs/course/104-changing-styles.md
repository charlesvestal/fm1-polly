# 104 Changing styles

Play your song in another style, and choose whose tempo it plays at.

[![104 Changing styles, the video (1 minute)](../video/104.jpg)](../video/104.mp4)

<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>1. Set up a song</h3>On Home, press <b>HOME</b> for the Song page. Press white keys 1 and 2.<br><br>Turn <b>SELECT</b> to page 2 and <b>KNOB 4</b> (At end) to again. Press <b>PLAY</b>. The song plays round.</td><td><img src="img/104/1-song.jpg" alt="The Song page: P1 Pop, P2 Ballad, At end again" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>2. Change the style</h3>Press <b>HOME</b> for Home. Turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Style) to Funk.<br><br>The song keeps its sections and chords. The band and the tempo are Funk's.</td><td><img src="img/104/2-style.jpg" alt="STYLE FUNK, tempo 100, section 1 of 2 playing" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>3. Set your own tempo</h3>Turn <b>KNOB 2</b> (Tempo) to 90. Turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Style) to Rock.<br><br>Rock's band plays, and your tempo stays.</td><td><img src="img/104/3-tempo.jpg" alt="TEMPO KEPT 90 (ROCK 120)" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>4. Use the style's tempo</h3>Press <b>SEL</b>. The list opens on STYLE TEMPO 120. Press <b>SEL</b> again.<br><br>From now on, each style brings its tempo. Turn <b>KNOB 1</b> back to Funk, and the tempo goes to 100.</td><td><img src="img/104/4-styletempo.jpg" alt="The list on STYLE TEMPO 120" width="100%"></td></tr>
