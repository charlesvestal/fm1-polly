# 105 Recording a melody

Record a melody along a short song, punch in on a section, and add a second melody.

Recording waits for the Intro to end.</td><td><img src="img/105/2-arm.jpg" alt="RECORD M1 INTO THE SONG: REC M1 after the Intro" width="100%"></td></tr>

The Ending plays, then the song stops.</td><td><img src="img/105/3-line.jpg" alt="REC M1 in section 2: the notes on the song's line and in the four bars" width="100%"></td></tr>

The click counts in one bar, then the song starts at section 2. Play a few notes, then press <b>REC</b> and <b>PLAY</b>.</td><td><img src="img/105/4-punch.jpg" alt="REC M1 in section 2 with the grid off: the new notes flash their bar" width="100%"></td></tr>
