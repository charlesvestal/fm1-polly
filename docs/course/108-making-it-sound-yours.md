# 108 Making it sound yours

Change an instrument's sound, mix it, set the effects and move the key.

[![108 Making it sound yours, the video (1 minute)](../video/108.jpg)](../video/108.mp4)

<table>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>1. Open the Sound page</h3>Press <b>PLAY</b>. Press <b>EDIT</b> for the Sound page. It opens on C1, the band's piano.</td><td><img src="img/108/1-instrument.jpg" alt="The Sound page on C1, Acoustic Grand" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>2. Pick a sound</h3>Turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Sound) up to Rock Organ. The piano part now plays on an organ.<br><br>The line above the knobs now reads Groove A (yours): your change goes into your own copy of the groove.</td><td><img src="img/108/2-sound.jpg" alt="KNOB 1 (Sound) turned to Rock Organ, Groove A (yours)" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>3. Mix it</h3>Press <b>LFO</b> for the Mixer, on C1. Turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Volume) to 100, and <b>KNOB 2</b> (Pan) to R10.</td><td><img src="img/108/3-mix.jpg" alt="The Mixer on C1: volume 100, pan R10" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>4. Add space</h3>Turn <b>KNOB 3</b> (Reverb) to 70 and <b>KNOB 4</b> (Chorus) to 40. They set how much C1 sends to the reverb and the chorus.</td><td><img src="img/108/4-sends.jpg" alt="C1's sends: reverb 70, chorus 40" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>5. Set the effects</h3>Press <b>FX</b> for the Effects page. Turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Reverb) to L.hall, the large hall, and <b>KNOB 2</b> (Level) to 80.<br><br>The bars show what each instrument sends.</td><td><img src="img/108/5-effects.jpg" alt="Effects: reverb L.hall, level 80" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>6. Move the key</h3>Press <b>HOME</b> for the Home page, then turn <b>KNOB 3</b> (Transpose) to D. Play a C chord. The band plays D.</td><td><img src="img/108/6-transpose.jpg" alt="Transpose D, a C chord played: the band plays D" width="100%"></td></tr>
</table>
