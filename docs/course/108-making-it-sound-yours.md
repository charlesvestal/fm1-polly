# 108 Making it sound yours

Change an instrument's sound, mix it, set the effects and move the key.

[![108 Making it sound yours, the video (1 minute)](../video/108.jpg)](../video/108.mp4)

Polly shows GROOVE A NOW YOURS: your changes go into your own copy of the groove.<br><br>Turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Sound) through the family to Rock Organ.</td><td><img src="img/108/2-sound.jpg" alt="KNOB 1 turned: Rock Organ in the organ family" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>3. Mix it</h3>Press <b>LFO</b> for the Mixer. Press black key 6 to select C2.<br><br>Turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Volume) to 80, and <b>KNOB 2</b> (Pan) to R10.</td><td><img src="img/108/3-mix.jpg" alt="The Mixer on C2: volume 80, pan R10" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>4. Add space</h3>Turn <b>KNOB 3</b> (Reverb) to 70 and <b>KNOB 4</b> (Chorus) to 60. They set how much C2 sends to the reverb and the chorus.</td><td><img src="img/108/4-sends.jpg" alt="C2's sends: reverb 70, chorus 60" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>5. Set the effects</h3>Press <b>FX</b> for the Effects page. Turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Reverb) to L.hall, the large hall, and <b>KNOB 2</b> (Level) to 80.<br><br>The bars show what each instrument sends.</td><td><img src="img/108/5-effects.jpg" alt="Effects: Large hall, level 80" width="100%"></td></tr>
