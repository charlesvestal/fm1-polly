# 102 Your own progressions

Record your own chords, fix one, change the length, name it, and switch progressions from any page.

Picks up where [101](101-playing-live.md) left off, with Polly stopped on the Home page. Any style works.

[![Watch 102 Your own progressions (1 minute)](../video/102.jpg)](../video/102.mp4)
