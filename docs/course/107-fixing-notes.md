# 107 Fixing notes

Change, move, delete and enter single notes, without recording again.

Starts where [106](106-your-own-grooves.md) ended: Polly stopped on the Band page, with Funk as the style.

[![107 Fixing notes, the video (1 minute)](../video/107.jpg)](../video/107.mp4)

<table>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>1. Open Notes</h3>Press <b>PLAY</b>. On the Band page, turn <b>ALGORITHM</b> to BA, the bass, then press <b>SEQ</b>.<br><br>Notes shows the bass's notes as a piano roll.</td><td><img src="img/107/1-notes.jpg" alt="Notes on BA, the first note selected" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>2. Pick a note</h3>Turn <b>SELECT</b>. It steps from note to note. The selected note is ringed in coral, and the line at the top names it.<br><br>Come back to the first note, C2 at bar 1.1.</td><td><img src="img/107/2-pick.jpg" alt="C2 at bar 1.1 selected" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>3. Change its pitch</h3>Turn <b>KNOB 2</b> (Pitch) one step. Here the bass plays the chord's root, so Polly asks "BASS PLAYS THE ROOT HERE. AS WRITTEN?"<br><br>Press <b>SEL</b> (yes), and the bass plays your notes as written. Keep turning to E2.</td><td><img src="img/107/3-ask.jpg" alt="KNOB 2 turned: BASS PLAYS THE ROOT HERE. AS WRITTEN?" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>4. Change its length and velocity</h3>Turn <b>KNOB 3</b> (Length) up to make the note longer. Turn <b>KNOB 4</b> (Velocity) down to play it softer.</td><td><img src="img/107/3-change.jpg" alt="E2, 17/24 beats long, velocity 106" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>5. Move it</h3>Hold <b>SEL</b> and turn <b>SELECT</b>. The note moves one grid step per detent.</td><td><img src="img/107/4-move.jpg" alt="SEL held, SELECT turned: the note at 1.1.2" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>6. Delete a note, enter one</h3>Press <b>EDIT</b>. The selected note is deleted.<br><br>Turn <b>KNOB 1</b> (Place) to an empty place, then play a key. The note goes in there.</td><td><img src="img/107/5-enter.jpg" alt="A key played at bar 1.1: G3 entered" width="100%"></td></tr>
<tr><td width="38%" valign="top"><h3>7. Undo</h3>Hold <b>HOME</b> and press <b>REC</b>. The last edit is undone, and the header says what.<br><br>Press <b>PLAY</b> to stop.</td><td><img src="img/107/6-undo.jpg" alt="HOME held, REC pressed: UNDO GROOVE A NOTES" width="100%"></td></tr>
</table>
