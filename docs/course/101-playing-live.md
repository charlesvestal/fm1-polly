# 101 Playing live

Pick a style, play chords, and the band follows.

[![101 Playing live, the video (1 minute)](../video/101.jpg)](../video/101.mp4)

## 1. Pick a style

On Home, turn **KNOB 1** (Style). Try Funk.

![Turning KNOB 1 to Funk](img/101/1-style.jpg)

## 2. Play

Press **PLAY**, then hold three keys or more. Polly names the chord and the band follows it.

The chord stays when you let go, so play the next one whenever you like. Try Em7 and A7, two bars each.

![Em7 held, the band playing Groove A](img/101/2-play.jpg)

## 3. Go busier

Hold **HOME** and press black key 4. At the next bar, a fill plays, then the busier groove, Groove B.

![HOME held, black key 4 pressed: NEXT FILL AB](img/101/3-busier.jpg)

## 4. Come back down

Hold **HOME** and press black key 4 again. A fill plays, then Groove A again.

![HOME held, black key 4 pressed: NEXT FILL BA](img/101/4-down.jpg)

## 5. End it

Hold **HOME** and press black key 5. The Ending plays, then Polly stops by itself.

![HOME held, black key 5 pressed: NEXT ENDING](img/101/5-end.jpg)
