# 101 Playing live

Pick a style, play chords, and the band follows. Nothing here is recorded. That starts in 102.

[![Playing live on Polly, step by step (1 minute)](../video/101.jpg)](../video/101.mp4)

*The five steps below, from a fresh start. Each step pauses so you can follow along.*

## 1. Pick a style

On Home, turn **KNOB 1** (Style). Try Funk.

## 2. Play

Press **PLAY**, then hold three keys or more. Polly names the chord and the band follows it.

The chord stays when you let go, so play the next one whenever you like. Try Em7 and A7, two bars each.

## 3. Go busier

Hold **HOME** and press black key 4. At the next bar, a fill plays, then the busier groove, Groove B.

## 4. Come back down

Hold **HOME** and press black key 4 again. A fill plays, then Groove A again.

## 5. End it

Hold **HOME** and press black key 5. The Ending plays, then Polly stops by itself.
