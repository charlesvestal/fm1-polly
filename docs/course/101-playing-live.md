# 101 Playing live

Play chords and let the band follow. You'll pick a style, start with an Intro, switch to the busier groove
with a fill, come back, and end with an Ending. Nothing here is recorded. That starts in 102.

[![Playing live on Polly, step by step (1 minute)](../video/101.jpg)](../video/101.mp4)

*The six steps below, from a fresh start. Each step pauses so you can follow along.*

## 1. Pick a style

On Home, turn **KNOB 1** (Style). Try Funk. Each style brings its own tempo.

More: [Picking a style](../../MANUAL.md#picking-a-style).

## 2. Start with an Intro

Hold **HOME** and press black key 1. Polly shows that PLAY will start with the Intro. Play a chord, such as
Em7, then press **PLAY**. The Intro holds that chord, then Groove A starts.

Without this step, **PLAY** starts straight on Groove A.

More: [Intros and endings hold a chord](../../MANUAL.md#intros-and-endings-hold-a-chord).

## 3. Play chords

Hold three keys or more. Polly names the chord and the band follows it. The chord stays when you let go, so
play the next one whenever you like. Try Em7 and A7, two bars each.

More: [Playing chords](../../MANUAL.md#playing-chords), with every chord Polly knows.

## 4. Go busier

Hold **HOME** and press black key 4. At the next bar, a one-bar fill plays, then Groove B, the busier groove.
Black key 3 goes straight to Groove B, without a fill.

## 5. Come back down

Press black key 4 again (with **HOME** held). From Groove B, the fill leads back to Groove A. Whatever you ask
for starts at the next bar.

More: [Changing grooves](../../MANUAL.md#changing-grooves).

## 6. End it

Hold **HOME** and press black key 5. The Ending plays at the next bar, then Polly stops by itself. Or press
**PLAY** to stop at once.

## Also good to know

- **Tempo**: turn **KNOB 2** on Home, or hold **HOME** and turn **SELECT** on any page. See
  [Tempo](../../MANUAL.md#tempo).
- **Holding HOME** shows all of this on screen: the five black keys and what they play.
