Polly Mirror

the FM-1's screen and panel, live over USB: view only

Not connected.

How

  1. Plug the FM-1 running Polly into this computer.
  2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge (Web MIDI), press Connect and allow MIDI with SysEx.
  3. Polly is picked by itself; else choose its ports (Polly FM-1) above.

What you see

Every screen update, as Polly draws it. Buttons and keys light as their LEDs do; the ones held are outlined in teal; a knob turned flashes and shows its detents. Nothing here plays the FM-1: it only watches. Unplugged or asleep, the page waits and picks it up again.

Speed

The screen goes run-length coded over USB-MIDI, after the notes and the clock. A slow moment (a busy host) skips frames, never the picture: Polly sends what you are missing as soon as there is room.
