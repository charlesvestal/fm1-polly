QY70 styles

Your ROM stays on your computer; Polly builds its QY70 styles here, in your browser. Pick or drop the QY70's main ROM (MAME's qy70main.bin): its 128 preset styles then follow Polly's own 16 on the Style knob, and this browser keeps them for your next visit.

How it plays

Polly plays styles: a pattern for each section (Intro, Main A and B, Fills, Ending) on eight tracks, following the chord you play. Press PLAY, then change chords on the keys. The screen shows the page you are on; the four knobs under it act on what the page shows. MIDI in (USB) plays all 16 channels. With QY70 styles loaded above, Setup (GLO) shows how many there are.

The controls, as the firmware lists them

Mouse and touch

  • Drag a knob up or down, or use the scroll wheel over it.
  • Click or touch buttons and keys. Several fingers at once work on a touch screen.
  • Shift-click (or right-click) a button to hold it down, click again to let go: for SEL + a key.
  • Hover over a control for what it does.

Keyboard

  • White keys 1–8: Q–I, 9–16: A–K. Black keys 1–11: 1–0, -.
  • Space PLAY, Esc HOME, Z / X OCT− / OCT+.
  • Up / down arrow keys turn PRESETS; left / right turn ALGORITHM.

Saving

Saving keeps your songs, patterns and settings in this browser, as the FM-1 keeps them in its memory. Factory reset forgets them (not the QY70 styles: Forget does that). It is the firmware, not a recording: what you hear is Polly running, at the FM-1's 44.1 kHz.
